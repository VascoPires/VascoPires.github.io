Why do chains sag?

A hanging chain turns a small change in length into a visible change in sag. Explore its initial shape and how an assumed creep law changes it over time.

The shape of a hanging chain
Analytical catenary equations and a length constraint connect support spacing, chain length, and sag.
Length change over time
An assumed creep law adds length under sustained load. Re-solving the catenary shows how sag evolves between fixed supports.

Stay down.

A lunar rover reaches the ground. The legs can store the impact energy, but where does that energy go next?

Follow the damped touchdown and its solved response, then take the challenge from energy absorption to leg strength and buckling.

Approaching the surface
